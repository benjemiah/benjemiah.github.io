Speeding through October and the stress of everyday life i catched myself at the gym, writing my current set and reps progress into the notes app. Annoyed i thought to myself "there must be a better way to handle this". I sat down after getting home and vibecoded a little bit. 
I researched iOS programming and discovered what a pain in the ass it is. Turns out without a subscription to the Apple Developer Program, everything is just a workaround. I settled for the following option: hosting the app via github pages as a mobile (web) app and creating a direct link on my homescreen to access it. Works like a charm!

Check it out: [Open Link](https://benjemiah.github.io/trainlog/)

I feel like the usage is self-explaining but i may write a documentation in the future. I want to note that this is solely for my own usage and enjoyment, im not trying to commercialize this. Its rough around the edges but it works and thats what matters to me! The (mobile) app is saving your workouts locally, so you wont see my workouts when accessing the website. So you'd have to be careful with clearing browser data!
