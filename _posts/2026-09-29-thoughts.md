# Thoughts

In the wake of current circumstance, i dove deeper into RF electronics. I found Marki Mikrowaves LC Filter Design Tool [Open Link](https://markimicrowave.com/technical-resources/tools/lc-filter-design-tool/), which makes life easier. I have tested some very broad bandwith LC-Filter structures for my CB-Radio project.
One thing i learned is that it is not as easy as i thought to design and build a LC Filter for such frequencies. You have to look out for the impedance of the PCB and the varying capacities and inductances of your components. At 27Mhz this is more cricitcal than i thought...

But we persist.
My next step is building a LC Filter (Lowpass, Butterworth or Chebyshev, 7th Order, Cutoff at 30-40 Mhz) and checking the passband ripple; in the LTspice simulation i noticed Gain in the passband, which of course isn't possible for a passive LC-Filter.

Further on i need to build a clock distribution system on a similar frequency. 

Exciting Times!
